The problem

Nobody aligns the listener, the creator, and the ad

Every major podcast app fails listeners somewhere. Apple's playback and queue are messy, Spotify buries podcasts and paywalls downloads, YouTube has no real "continue listening" and paywalls background audio. Each is optimizing for a business model, an app store, a music subscription, an ad exchange, that points away from the listener.

Underneath that, the economics are misaligned. Host-read ads are the format listeners tolerate and creators depend on, but they have always been hard to measure, so the money keeps drifting toward formats that serve the platform instead of the show. I wanted to design the product where what delights a listener and what grows a creator's business finally point the same direction.

What I did

Decided in writing, then built it for real

Worked backwards from a press release

I wrote an Amazon-style PR-FAQ as a real press article to force clarity on the value before designing a single screen, scoping to two in-app customers, listeners and creators, with advertiser value flowing through existing ad products rather than a new console.

Designed the signature moves

Sample any show from an auto-generated clip and swipe a Shorts-style feed to discover; a real save-for-later and a resume you can trust; a tappable, host-read "offer card" that makes a spoken ad measurable without ever stopping the audio.

Handed discovery back to the listener

A "Your Algorithm" panel that lets people see and steer their own recommendations, glass-box discovery in a category built on black boxes, so control sits with the person listening rather than the platform.

Grounded it in the business

Host-reads are the majority of a multi-billion-dollar US podcast ad market and convert far better than programmatic, but have always been hard to attribute. The offer card turns them into a clean first-party signal, with creators keeping RSS ownership and no walled garden.

Built it for real

A working progressive web app on live RSS feeds: real playback, queue, continue-listening, offline and installable, a Podcast Shorts clip feed, offer cards, a Listener⇄Creator toggle, and a creator dashboard with an ad-load dial that trades revenue against listener experience in real time.

Outcome

A usable product, not a mockup

OpenSignal carries the "listeners and creators on the same wavelength" thesis from end to end: from market analysis and a working-backwards PR through to a deployed app you can actually use. Every control is real and interactive. It's the single clearest proof of how I work, start from the customer outcome, make the business case unmistakable, then ship something real.
